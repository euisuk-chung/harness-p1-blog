Title: Staged Deliberation for Long-Horizon Reasoning

Abstract:
We introduce staged deliberation, a method that separates plan generation from
plan execution using an explicit intermediate artifact. Rather than emitting a
single chain of thought, the model writes a structured plan, revises it once
against a checklist, and only then executes. On held-out long-horizon tasks the
method achieves state-of-the-art on English benchmarks while reducing token cost
by 31 percent compared to self-consistency decoding. We do not evaluate on
non-English tasks and leave multilingual analysis to future work.
