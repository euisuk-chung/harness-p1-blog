Title: On the Fragility of Tool-Use Benchmarks

Abstract:
Tool-use benchmarks are increasingly used to rank agent systems. We show that
small changes to tool descriptions shift measured accuracy by up to 12 points
without any change to the underlying model. We therefore caution that reported
tool-use scores are not comparable across papers unless the exact tool schema is
published alongside the result.
